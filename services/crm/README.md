# CRM Module (AI-Ready)

This module provides foundational CRM features, ready for AI enhancements:

- Manage customers and contacts
- Track opportunities & sales pipeline
- Coordinate tasks and follow-ups in the same workflow
- Integrate with financial data for cross-insights
- Enable AI-powered lead scoring and churn prediction

## Sample Features
- Customer & contact management
- Opportunity tracking (status, value, stage)
- Task management (assignment, priority, status, due dates)
- Notes, activities, and follow-ups tied to tasks or deals
- AI: Lead scoring, churn risk, upsell suggestions

---

### Usage

Use the `Customer`, `Opportunity`, `Task`, and `CRMReport` models to build CRM + task features and analytics. Extend with your own endpoints or AI functions.
